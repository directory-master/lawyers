About lawyers in Tucker

Tucker is in DeKalb County, and most cases are heard at the DeKalb County courthouse, part of the Stone Mountain Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. Tucker is in DeKalb County; cases go to the DeKalb courts in Decatur. Several Tucker firms focus on immigration for the area’s large immigrant community.

We list 74 lawyers and law firms in Tucker: 56 firms and 18 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (49), personal injury (13), divorce & family (4), real estate (1) and immigration (4).
