About lawyers in Norcross

Norcross is in Gwinnett County, and most cases are heard at the Gwinnett County courthouse, part of the Gwinnett Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. Norcross is in Gwinnett County; cases are heard in Lawrenceville, and many Norcross firms serve Spanish speaking clients.
