About lawyers in Augusta

Augusta is the county seat of Richmond County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Augusta Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, Augusta Division. Augusta has a federal courthouse and serves the Central Savannah River Area, including Columbia County and the South Carolina side of the river for federal matters.

We list 189 lawyers and law firms in Augusta: 117 firms and 72 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (102), personal injury (44), divorce & family (9), criminal defense (12) and real estate (6). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Jamie Casino Injury Attorneys - Augusta Personal Injury Lawyer currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 607 reviews.
