About lawyers in Columbus

Columbus is the county seat of Muscogee County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Chattahoochee Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, Columbus Division. Columbus lawyers serve Muscogee and the surrounding counties, and the community around Fort Moore, with a strong share of military family and personal injury work.

We list 156 lawyers and law firms in Columbus: 82 firms and 74 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (92), personal injury (32), divorce & family (11), criminal defense (10) and real estate (1). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Valerie Long, Attorney at Law currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 299 reviews.
