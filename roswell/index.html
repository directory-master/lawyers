About lawyers in Roswell

Roswell is in Fulton County, and most cases are heard at the Fulton County courthouse, part of the Atlanta Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. Roswell is in North Fulton County, so felony and major civil cases go to the Fulton County courts in Atlanta while traffic and ordinance cases stay in Roswell Municipal Court.

We list 112 lawyers and law firms in Roswell: 90 firms and 22 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (63), personal injury (21), divorce & family (9), criminal defense (6) and real estate (4). Together they average 4.6 stars from public reviews. Melman Law Group currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 823 reviews.
