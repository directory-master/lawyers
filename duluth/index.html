About lawyers in Duluth

Duluth is in Gwinnett County, and most cases are heard at the Gwinnett County courthouse, part of the Gwinnett Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. Duluth is in Gwinnett County; cases are heard in Lawrenceville. Many Duluth lawyers serve Korean, Chinese and Spanish speaking clients.

We list 153 lawyers and law firms in Duluth: 102 firms and 51 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (81), personal injury (32), divorce & family (5), criminal defense (5) and real estate (7). Together they average 4.6 stars from public reviews. Keith D. Leshine Attorney At Law currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 572 reviews.
