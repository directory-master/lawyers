About lawyers in Marietta

Marietta is the county seat of Cobb County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Cobb Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. The Cobb County courts on the Marietta square are among the busiest in the state, and many firms keep offices along Roswell Street and Washington Avenue nearby.

We list 305 lawyers and law firms in Marietta: 202 firms and 103 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (132), personal injury (53), divorce & family (45), criminal defense (44) and real estate (9). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Jones & Swanson currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 569 reviews.
