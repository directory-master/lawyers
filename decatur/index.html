About lawyers in Decatur

Decatur is the county seat of DeKalb County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Stone Mountain Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. The DeKalb County Courthouse on the Decatur square is one of the busiest in Georgia, and many lawyers keep offices within walking distance of it.

We list 201 lawyers and law firms in Decatur: 137 firms and 64 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (102), personal injury (46), divorce & family (18), criminal defense (11) and real estate (2). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Monge & Associates Injury and Accident Attorneys currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 345 reviews.
