About lawyers in Newnan

Newnan is the county seat of Coweta County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Coweta Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Newnan Division. Newnan has a federal courthouse and serves Coweta, Carroll, Heard, Meriwether and Troup counties.

We list 83 lawyers and law firms in Newnan: 51 firms and 32 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (40), personal injury (11), divorce & family (9), criminal defense (4) and real estate (6). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Trammell Injury Law currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 110 reviews.
