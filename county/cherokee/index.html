About lawyers in Cherokee County

Canton is the county seat of Cherokee County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Blue Ridge Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 147 lawyers and law firms across Canton (73), Woodstock (71), Holly Springs (1), Lathemtown (1) and Waleska (1): 97 firms and 50 solo attorneys. Capovilla & Williams in Woodstock currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 441 reviews.
