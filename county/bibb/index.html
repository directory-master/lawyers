About lawyers in Bibb County

Macon is the county seat of Bibb County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Macon Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, Macon Division.

We list 247 lawyers and law firms across Macon (247): 132 firms and 115 solo attorneys. Brodie Law Group in Macon currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 353 reviews.
