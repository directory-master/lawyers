About lawyers in DeKalb County

Decatur is the county seat of DeKalb County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Stone Mountain Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 417 lawyers and law firms across Decatur (201), Tucker (74), Dunwoody (43), Stone Mountain (33), Chamblee (18) and Doraville (14) and 7 more cities: 284 firms and 133 solo attorneys. The JW Law Firm, LLC in Tucker currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 1,096 reviews.
