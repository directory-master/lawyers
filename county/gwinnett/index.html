About lawyers in Gwinnett County

Lawrenceville is the county seat of Gwinnett County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Gwinnett Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 689 lawyers and law firms across Lawrenceville (180), Duluth (153), Norcross (104), Suwanee (65), Peachtree Corners (60) and Snellville (48) and 6 more cities: 497 firms and 192 solo attorneys.
