About lawyers in Fayette County

Fayetteville is the county seat of Fayette County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Griffin Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 134 lawyers and law firms across Fayetteville (75), Peachtree City (52) and Tyrone (7): 92 firms and 42 solo attorneys. The Clifton Law Firm, LLC in Fayetteville currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 74 reviews.
