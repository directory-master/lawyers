About lawyers in Chatham County

Savannah is the county seat of Chatham County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Eastern Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, Savannah Division.

We list 246 lawyers and law firms across Savannah (227), Pooler (15), Garden City (3) and Thunderbolt (1): 170 firms and 76 solo attorneys. The Atlantic Law Firm in Savannah currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 744 reviews.
