About lawyers in Glynn County

Brunswick is the county seat of Glynn County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Brunswick Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, Brunswick Division.

We list 130 lawyers and law firms across Brunswick (95) and St Simons Island (35): 67 firms and 63 solo attorneys. Boyd Law Firm, LLC in Brunswick currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 466 reviews.
