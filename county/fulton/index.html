About lawyers in Fulton County

Atlanta is the county seat of Fulton County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Atlanta Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 1,828 lawyers and law firms across Atlanta (1,350), Alpharetta (217), Roswell (112), Sandy Springs (62), Johns Creek (30) and College Park (29) and 6 more cities: 1,334 firms and 494 solo attorneys.
