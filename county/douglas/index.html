About lawyers in Douglas County

Douglasville is the county seat of Douglas County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Douglas Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 76 lawyers and law firms across Douglasville (68) and Lithia Springs (8): 47 firms and 29 solo attorneys. Arrowhead Clinic - Lithia Springs in Lithia Springs currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 1,038 reviews.
