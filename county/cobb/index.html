About lawyers in Cobb County

Marietta is the county seat of Cobb County, and the county’s Superior, Magistrate and Probate courts sit there, part of the Cobb Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division.

We list 440 lawyers and law firms across Marietta (305), Kennesaw (58), Smyrna (28), Acworth (16), Austell (14) and Powder Springs (9) and 3 more cities: 299 firms and 141 solo attorneys. Jones & Swanson in Marietta currently ranks first with 5.0 stars across 569 reviews.
