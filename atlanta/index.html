About lawyers in Atlanta

Atlanta is the county seat of Fulton County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Atlanta Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Atlanta Division. Atlanta is also home to the Supreme Court of Georgia, the Court of Appeals, the Richard B. Russell federal courthouse and the Atlanta Immigration Court, so nearly every kind of legal practice is represented here.

We list 1,350 lawyers and law firms in Atlanta: 968 firms and 382 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (624), personal injury (359), divorce & family (94), criminal defense (84) and real estate (45). Together they average 4.6 stars from public reviews. Holston & Huntley Trial Attorneys currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 1,633 reviews.
