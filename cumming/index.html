About lawyers in Cumming

Cumming is the county seat of Forsyth County, so the county’s Superior, State, Magistrate and Probate courts sit here, part of the Bell-Forsyth Judicial Circuit. Federal cases go to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, Gainesville Division. Forsyth County is one of the fastest growing counties in the state, and its lawyers see a heavy mix of real estate closings, divorce and custody cases, DUI and traffic matters from GA 400.

We list 116 lawyers and law firms in Cumming: 76 firms and 40 solo attorneys, with the most practicing general practice (39), personal injury (24), divorce & family (21), criminal defense (10) and real estate (14). Together they average 4.5 stars from public reviews. Bascom Law, P.C. currently holds the top spot with 5.0 stars across 839 reviews.
